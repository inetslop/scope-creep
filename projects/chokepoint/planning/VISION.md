# Vision — Chokepoint

**Pitch:** A white road winds across a light island, and shapes are coming down it. Every run is a new board. Towers have no types: they are what you put in them. Pick an augment, build a tower with it beside the road, and after every wave pick another and combine: a sniper that slows, exploding needles, a frost pulse that also shoots. Gold buys more slots, each dearer, so you choose between many simple towers and a few strong combos. Let enough through and it's over; see how many waves you can hold.

**Genre:** Roguelite tower defence. Short runs (10-25 minutes), endless escalating waves, a new board and new choices every run.

**Platforms:** Desktop and mobile browsers, equally. Everything is a click or a tap; nothing needs precise aim or speed.

## Pillars

1. **Every placement is a decision.** Cells beside a bend are worth more than cells beside a straight; gold is always short. The board, not a tutorial, teaches where to build.
2. **Every run is different.** A new board from the seed, different augment offers, different enemy mixes: builds that feel different from run to run, not one optimal layout.
3. **Readable at a glance.** Shape and color carry meaning: enemies are warm (red, orange, purple); a tower takes its first augment's shape and color and shows every augment in it (barrels, rings, ticks). You can always see what is coming, what each tower does and reaches, and what you can afford.
4. **Bright minimal, abstract and neutral.** Like Mini Motorways: a soft light ground, the board as a raised island with a white road, chunky rounded tokens with a darker side face and soft shadows, white floating cards for the HUD, rounded bold type. No theme (no fantasy, sci-fi, ink or paper dressing), no outlines, no glow, no gradients, no image files.
5. **Juice through form.** Towers drop in with a bounce and kick when they fire, enemies pop in and flash when hit, kills burst into rounded chips, gold pops up where it is earned, the camera jolts on a leak.
6. **Plays great on a phone.** Portrait or landscape, one finger, large touch targets, 60 fps on a mid-range phone.
7. **Depth over count.** A new enemy brings a behavior no other enemy has; a new augment brings an attack of its own and a change that combines with the others in a new way. Never more of the same with other numbers.
8. **Nothing unexplained.** Every key, button and HUD element is shown where it is used and listed on the pause screen.

## Tone

Calm and precise: short plain names (*Bolt*, *Pulse*, *Dot*, *Dart*, *Block*, *Sharpen*), dry one-line descriptions, no lore.
