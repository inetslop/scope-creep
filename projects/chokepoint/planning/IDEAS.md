# Ideas (scope-creep backlog)

The PO adds ideas here. Good ones become tasks. The bar: a new enemy needs a behavior no existing enemy has; a new augment needs an attack of its own (as a tower's first) and a change that combines in a new way (when added). Never a copy with other numbers.

- Sell a tower for 70% of what its slots cost (tap it, then Sell)
- Take an augment back out of a tower into the hand, for a fee
- Lance augment: first, a long piercing line that hits everything along it; added, shots pierce one more enemy — built as T0004 (asus)
- Chain augment: first, a zap that jumps between nearby enemies; added, hits jump to one more enemy — tasked as T0025 (asus, cycle 8)
- Push augment: first, a gust that pushes everything within 2.5 cells back along the road, no damage (a support pulse, like Frost's slow and Mark's mark); added, hits push struck enemies back 0.4 cells (+0.4 per Push) — the first augment that moves enemies along the road instead of slowing or stopping them — tasked as T0027 (asus, cycle 9)
- Gust wind-up: a thin ring around a Push tower's base fills as the next gust charges, so the next shove is readable at a glance (small, after T0027)
- Mark augment: first, marks the enemy furthest along so it takes 25% more damage; added, hits mark too — built as T0012 (asus)
- Rare augments that change a rule (e.g. towers next to each other share their slows)
- Splitter enemy: breaks into two smaller ones when it dies
- Shield enemy: a ring that absorbs the first hits for its neighbours
- Sprinter enemy: speeds up each time it is hit — built as T0006 (desktop)
- Flyer: ignores the path and crosses the board in a straight line (only some towers can hit it)
- Boss every 10 waves with a health bar and its own mechanic
- Wave preview strip in the build phase: the shapes and counts of the next wave — tasked as T0015 (in flight)
- Interest: unspent gold at the end of a build phase earns 5% (capped)
- Fast-forward button (2x speed) for the wave phase — tasked as T0019 (narcissus, cycle 4)
- Daily seeded run with a shareable result
- Colorblind mode: patterns on the shapes, not just color
- Map modifiers chosen in the draft instead of an augment (e.g. "the next wave comes from both ends")
- Haptics on mobile for builds and leaks
- Draft reroll: once per run, reroll the three offered cards for 15 gold — tasked as T0036 (cycle 16)
- Leak warning: the last 3 seconds before an enemy reaches the exit, the exit pulses red with a quiet tick — built as T0014 (yoga)
- Ghost run: a menu option to replay your best run's exact board (same seed) and beat your own wave record
- Slow zone: a light blue patch on the board slows enemies crossing it by 30%
- DPS line in the tower panel: a damage-per-second number per tower, updated as augments are added
- First-run hints: on the very first run only (profile runsPlayed = 0), short hints on the board ("build beside the road"), dismissed by doing it
- Sound on/off toggle on the pause screen, remembered in the profile — tasked as T0035 (cycle 16)
- Wave banner: at wave start a small card slides in at the top — "Wave 8" — and fades after ~1s
- Kill combo: kills within 1.5 s chain a rising "×2, ×3" counter above the last kill — built as T0031 (yoga)
- Draft reveal: the three draft cards flip over when the draft opens (200 ms), Epic cards flip a little longer — built as T0024 (yoga)
- Rally augment (Epic): every tower within 2 cells of a Rally tower attacks 10% faster — rewards building in clusters — built as T0020 (asus)
- Mint augment (Epic): no attack of its own — every kill within 2 cells of a Mint tower drops +1 extra gold; added, the host tower's kills drop +1 extra gold too; closest is Frost (a passive aura), new is that it changes the economy, not combat — tasked as T0021 (asus, cycle 6)
- Wild card: one draft card is a wildcard that becomes the augment you pick, always at least Rare
- Leaker: a big slow enemy that costs 2 lives when it leaks — the first enemy that hurts you twice as much
- Recent runs: the menu lists your last few runs (wave + coins) so you can see your trend — built as T0018 (desktop)
- A "New best" star on the menu's profile card when your last run hit your best wave — built as T0016 (yoga)
- Mender enemy: a slow round enemy that heals nearby enemies in a pulsing ring; closest is Block, new is that it heals other enemies, which no current enemy does
- Anchor augment: first, roots (fully stops) everything within 2.5 cells for 0.75 s every 3 s; added, hits root for 0.5 s (+0.25 s per extra copy). Closest is Frost (a slow); new is a full timed stop that also applies when stacked on an attacking tower — tasked as T0023 (asus, cycle 7)
- Ember augment: first, sets ablaze the enemy furthest along every 1.5 s — 3/4/5 damage per second for 3 s by tier; re-ignition refreshes the duration and keeps the higher rate (never additive); the ticks are the source tower's damage, so Mark amplifies them and a killing tick is its kill for gold and stats; added, hits set ablaze at the tower's tier rate. Closest is Mark (a cadence effect on the furthest enemy); new is damage that keeps ticking after the hit — the first damage over time — tasked as T0028 (asus, cycle 10)
- Beam augment: first, a continuous beam on the enemy furthest along — 6/8/10 damage per second by tier, no shots, retargeting the instant its target dies or leaves range; added, a second beam on the enemy nearest the main target at half the per-second rate. Closest is Reach (a long single-target attack); new is the sustained line and the sustained second target on any first augment — tasked as T0029 (asus, cycle 10)
- Enemy telegraph: an enemy whose behavior is special (a Sprinter, a Marked enemy, a Splitter) shows a small neutral icon on itself while its ability matters, so new behaviors are readable at a glance
- Lives as a danger bar: the lives readout becomes a small bar that drifts toward red and pulses as you get close to losing your last life — was T0022 (rejected: the 196 px stats card has no room for 20 pips; would need single-digit starting lives or a growing card — revisit if either lands)
- Tower earnings: the tower panel shows how much gold a tower has earned this run, so you can see which towers are paying for themselves
- The clean wave's bonus shown during the build phase: a small line under the wave preview ("clean: +3 gold") — the clean chip (T0005) is in; waiting only on the wave preview (T0015, in flight)
- A run-so-far line on the pause screen: wave, lives, gold, coins earned this run — tasked as T0030 (yoga, cycle 10)
- The buy-slot button reads why it is quiet: when gold is short it dims and shows the price in red, so a first-time player sees the gap, not a dead button
- Tier word on the draft card: the name line of Rare and Epic cards reads "Name · Rare" / "Name · Epic" (Common stays plain), the same line the tower panel shows — built as T0026 (yoga)
- What-if preview: while an augment is held over a tower's free slot, the tower briefly shows the result (new barrel, ring or tick) before the gold is spent, so the decision is visible before it is paid for
- Tight-pack waves: some waves send enemies bunched close together (a "tight" tag in the wave preview, once T0015 is in) so multi-hit augments (Splash, Lance, Chain) shine
- A second pick: every 5th draft offers a 4th card
- Burn on death: when a burning enemy dies, its burn jumps to the nearest enemy within 2 cells (one jump, same rate) — a small rule after T0028
- Combo milestone: at ×10 the kill combo counter turns yellow and stays yellow for the rest of the chain, and the ×10 kill bursts with twice the chip count — T0031 is in — tasked as T0034 (yoga, cycle 15)
- Beam hum: a very quiet hum under an active beam, one per tower, fading in and out with the line — a small audio touch after T0029, only if it stays quiet with several beams at once — tasked as T0043 (cycle 23)
- Tier chime: on the draft reveal flip (T0024), each offered card lands with a short chime — one low note Common, two a third higher Rare, three the highest Epic, all at the soft UI-blip volume — so the tier is heard as well as seen — built as T0032 (yoga)
- Ricochet augment: first, a shot at the enemy furthest along that bounces off the first rock it hits once and strikes the nearest enemy it meets on the other side (same damage, same cadence as a Bolt shot); added, the shot bounces one more rock. Closest is Bolt (a straight shot at the furthest); new is that the board's rocks take part in the attack — the first augment that uses the board's shape in combat — tasked as T0038 (cycle 19; in queue, waits on T0029, T0034 and T0035)
- Dasher enemy: a small shape that crawls at half speed, pauses with a short tell, then dashes three cells in a flash; closest is Dart (a fast constant walker); new is burst movement on a cycle — slow while it telegraphs, dangerous while it dashes, so a fixed slow build can't pin it (Lead, T0040, is the tower-side answer once it lands)
- Streak: three clean waves in a row restore one life (the lives readout pops green), once per run — a perfect stretch pays back in survival, on top of the clean-wave coins
- Demo at your level: the menu's live demo runs a wave from around your saved best wave instead of always an early one, so the menu shows how hard the game has gotten — no input, no card
- Mute enemy: a slow walker that silences any tower within 2 cells while it is alive (that tower holds still, no attack; a jammed tower still counts for Rally's range); closest is Block (a plain walker); new is that it fights back — the first enemy that disables towers, and the one that makes cluster placement a real decision against Rally and Mint
- Lead augment: first, a slow heavy shot at the enemy furthest along that lands where the target will be (lead by the target's current speed); added, the shot leads one beat further per copy. Closest is Reach (slow, heavy, long range); new is the first predictive attack — it only matters once a Sprinter or Dasher is on the board — Sprinter is in (T0006) — tasked as T0040 (cycle 20; refined: lead = travel time × current speed, capped at 1.2 cells ahead so a still enemy is never missed, +0.6 per extra copy, added Lead leads aimed shots only)
- Working aura: a support tower (Rally, Mint, an Anchor with no attack) shimmers a quiet 1 s ring at its base each time its aura ticks, so you can see a tower that never fires is actually working — a small readability touch after T0020/T0021/T0023 land
- The run's seed on the rewards card: a short code (four letters) under the coins line, so a run can be shared and replayed on its exact board — the bridge between the daily seeded run and the ghost run; the rewards card has settled (T0033 in) — ready to task when the pool has room
- Resume a run: closing the browser mid-run keeps the run (board, towers, wave, gold) in the profile; the menu offers "Resume" beside Play, and "New run" abandons it for nothing — a mobile lifesaver for 10-25 minute runs; the save pattern is E1's — tasked as T0042 (cycle 23)
- Mint's gold pop: when a kill pays a Mint's +1 extra gold, a small gold "+1" pops up at the kill spot (the same pop as kill gold), so the aura's income is visible where it happens — a small juice after T0021 — tasked as T0039 (cycle 20; waits on T0021, T0028 and T0034 so it never builds on an in-flight edit to the kill flow)
- Wave-clear sweep: a soft two-note rising sweep (coin-chime family, same volume) in the beat between a wave ending and the draft opening, so the phase change is heard as well as seen — a small audio touch
- First boss: every 10th wave is a single big shape — six times Block's hp, Block's speed, 1 life on leak like anything else — with the run's first health bar (a small white card pinned to the top edge, centered, counting down as it is hit); at 50% hp it phases — translucent and untargetable for 2.5 s, every 8 s — so a pure single-target build needs a second line (Splash, Lance, Chain, Beam); closest existing enemy is Block, new is the timed untargetable phase — a survival behavior no enemy has — and the boss bar as a new HUD element; later bosses swap the mechanic (one shields its escort, one splits at half hp) — a bosses epic to task once E4's new augments are in
- Owned dot: a draft card whose augment is already in the hand shows a small dot on its top-right corner, so "stack this one" vs "branch this one" reads at a glance — small readability, no rule change
- Best-run star on the Recent line: the entry in the menu's Recent line (T0018) that reached your best wave gets the same small yellow star the profile card shows (T0016), so the trend line shows which run set the record — small, both T0018 and T0016 are in — built as T0037 (yoga)
- New-enemy name tag: the first time an enemy type appears in a run, a small white name label pops over it for ~1 s ("Sprinter"), so a shape's name is learned the moment it first matters — small readability, no rule change
- Draft never repeats: in one run the draft never offers the same augment twice in a row, so every pick always has at least one fresh branch to consider — a small rule change in the offer roll
- Hauler enemy: a fat slow round at Block's speed with a small fortune inside — killing it pays 10 gold (a few slots' worth for one kill); if it leaks you lose the life and 5 gold. Closest is Block (a plain slow walker); new is that the economy rides on it — the first enemy worth more dead than alive, and the first whose leak costs gold
- Panic pulse: a "Panic · 25" button under the stats card during a wave — 25 gold, once per wave, clears every enemy on the board in a white flash; dimmed while gold is short and between waves, "Panic · used" after the one use. The first time a player can buy their way out of a wave: a last gasp that costs gold they were saving for slots
- Quiet wave: every 4th wave is a breather — half the usual size, double the gold per kill — tagged "quiet" in the wave preview once T0015 is in, so the short build before it is a chance to spend, not a trap
- Epic frame shimmer: on the draft reveal flip (T0024), the Epic card's purple frame runs a one-shot 150 ms white shimmer, left to right, as it lands — so the rarest card is noticed before its name is read; a small visual touch, T0002 and T0024 are both in — tasked as T0041 (cycle 22)
- Kill gold trail: the gold earned on a kill slides from the kill spot to the HUD's gold tally as one small coin (about 200 ms), so the money is seen moving where it was earned — a small juice in the coin family (T0009, T0013, T0017 are in)
- First-draft floor: the pre-wave-1 draft always includes at least one Common Bolt among its three (a reroll used in draft 0 keeps the floor), so a first-time player's first tower is the game's default shot — the one that shoots the enemy furthest along — and from wave 2 on the roll is normal; a one-line rule in the offer roll, onboarding for pillar 3
- One life is loud: when exactly one life remains, the lives number in the stats card turns red and pulses once at the start of every wave — the leak warning (T0014) already covers the per-enemy danger; this makes the last life a state you see, not a number you count; a small juice, no layout change
- The panel says who's working: the tower panel gets a small muted-gray "kills N" line, so you can see which towers are doing the work this run; a small stat, no rule change — the first step toward a run summary
- Best combo on the claim: the rewards card gets a fifth line, "Best combo ×N", counting up with the others and green at ×10 and up (the milestone color, T0034) — the run's loudest moment remembered where the run is claimed; waits on T0034
- Score on the claim card: the rewards card's count-up gets a "Score N" line — the same score the end of the run shows today — so the number that ranks runs is seen where the run is claimed; small, E1, no rule change
- The quiet tower: a tower that has not fired in 3 s shows one small dimmed dot (the tier pips stay as they are), so a player sees at a glance that a tower is not working (nothing in range) instead of wondering why it is idle; the dot clears the moment it fires; small readability, no rule change
