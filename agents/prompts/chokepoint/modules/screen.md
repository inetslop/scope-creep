# The screens

Everything drawn over the board: the HUD's white cards and buttons; the draft, pause, game over and rewards overlays; the start screen and the tech tree. "On screen" in the inventory says what each HUD element shows.

## The bar
- **One in, one out.** Something new that stays on screen says what it replaces, or why the screen has room and the player needs it more than the space.
- **Nothing shown twice.** Check "On screen" first.
- **One control per action.** Never a second button for something an existing one does.
- **It fits.** Every line that can show at once, with the longest numbers, fits its card at 412x915 and 1280x720.

## Not in this game
The owner removed these. Don't propose them again, or anything that works the same way.
- A count of the enemies left above the exit (T0106): the next-wave button already shows it.
- Status chips over enemies for mark, burn, slow, ramp and root (T0179, T0192): the enemy's own look already shows them.
- A second start-wave button, "Send · +N" (T0223): the big button does it, and the early-send gold moved onto it.
- The wave preview's "+N gold" line (T0223): awkward, and not worth the room.
