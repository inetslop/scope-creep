# The board

The island a run is played on, generated from the seed every run: a white road from the entrance gate to the exit gate, build cells beside it, rocks that block cells. The player reads the road, the towers and the enemies on it; anything else on the board is noise.

## The bar
- Nothing is painted on the cells or the island to hint, teach or decorate. The player sees where to build from what is already there: the road, and the range ring while placing a tower.
- A change to how boards are made says what the player sees differently and why it makes placing towers more interesting.
