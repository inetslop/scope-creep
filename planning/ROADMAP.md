# Roadmap

The PO keeps this file current. Milestones are loose; scope creep is expected.

## M0 — Baseline ✅
- Playable loop: move, aim, shoot, enemies, shards, level-ups, game over, restart
- Twin-stick controls for keyboard+mouse, touch and gamepad
- 3 enemies, 2 weapons, 10 upgrades, procedural Bauhaus art
- Smoke-tested on desktop and mobile on every change

## M2 — Variety (consolidated 2026-10-27)
The agents had padded the game: 49 upgrades (35 of them pairs of existing stat bonuses), 18 enemies (11 of them chasers that differed only in numbers, shape or color) and two active abilities with unexplained keys and a bare HUD. All of it was cut by hand; see VISION pillars 6 and 7 and the content bar in the PO prompt. Variety now grows by mechanics, not by count.
- Enemies kept (behavior): Wedge (chase), Dart (weave), Block (charge), Sentry (ranged), Splitter (splits into Splinters), plus the bosses' minions. Platen — a heavy yellow circle that flattens and slams every 5 s, a 160-unit ink shockwave shoving the player and nearby enemies away (the slam deals no damage); the first enemy that pushes instead of chasing (T0091) ✅. The Hare — a small green circle that chases at 40 u/s and quickens to 52 / 67.6 / 87.9 as it loses each quarter of its max HP, so the kill you are chasing gets faster (T0103) ✅. The Wash — a pale blue circle (35 hp, 55 u/s, chase) that drops a 120-unit pale blue puddle every 2.0 s; each puddle lasts 2.0 s and slows the player 20% while inside; the first enemy to leave a persistent effect on the arena (T0111) ✅. After it, from IDEAS: The Retouch (healer), Type block (first falling enemy), Mine, Ring, Needle, Bracket, Ligature. Long-bar enemies (Tube 120×16, The Rail 160×12) are expressible now — the shape aspect landed (T0098) — but the pool is at the cap, so one of them must remove or merge an existing enemy first. Pool: 10/10 (at the cap — the Wash landed) — the next enemy must remove or merge an existing one (held: the retirement decision lands with the next enemy task — Wedge is the only redundant behavior but it is in the pitch's trinity; see the pool notes in IDEAS.md).
- Weapons: Point, Line, Orbit (T0008) ✅, Arc (T0022) ✅, Counterpoint wall-bounce (T0055) ✅, The Plumb — a thin ink line hangs from the top of the page at your aim's X and re-aims instantly; 12 damage per 0.5 s per enemy to anything that crosses it (a small 60-unit horizontal shove) and it eats the enemy bullets that touch it (T0119, in queue, desktop-4070), Fresco — while you move you leave a 40-unit ink trail behind you (patches stamped every 40 ms, 2.0 s life, fading) that deals 10 damage once per enemy to anything that crosses it (T0136, in queue, inet-racing — retires Draft, the pool's next scheduled departure); backlog: Stamp, Hatch, Baseline, Fanfare, negative-space bomb (each is a new level-up card — see the pool note under upgrades)
- Upgrades (16, at the cap): Rhythm, Weight, Repetition, Mass, Gravity, Vector, Balance, Composition, Contrast (crit), Draft (projectile speed), the Line, Orbit, Arc and Counterpoint (T0055) ✅ weapon cards, the Plumb weapon card (T0119, in queue — in for Momentum, which it retires) and the first conditional card, Underexposure (T0105) ✅ — any shape below 50% of its max HP takes double damage from you, measured on the HP before the hit; the threshold widens to 75% at level 2; it applies to elites and bosses and composes ×4 with crits, so the wounded shapes are the kill you end fastest. Next: more conditionals (Full Bleed, Scavenge, Sforzando, Overprint, Margin), weapon evolutions (Point → Bullet), Kerning (projectile size + knockback). Pool: 16/16 (at the cap) — a new card removes an existing one in the same task: T0119 retires Momentum (the +12% move-speed card) and T0136 (Fresco) retires Draft (the +25% projectile-speed card, the most situational of the remaining stat cards) — the two retire different cards and land in either order; after both, the next candidate is the following most-situational stat card (Composition or Balance). No new card is tasked this cycle: the pool file is hot while both retirements are in flight — the conditional candidates (Stride, Register, Margin) and the weapon backlog (Stamp, Hatch, Baseline, Fanfare) wait for them
- Actives: removed 2026-10-27. They may return only as an upgrade card that grants one or as a hero/loadout picked before the run, with the key shown on screen (desktop, gamepad, a touch button) and a designed cooldown element. The Redactor (T0124, see M4) is the first pre-run pick.
- Systems: Crop marks — after 12:00 the arena's safe area shrinks to 70% by 17:00; the cropped margin burns the player for 5 damage/s (T0045) ✅
- Inkwell: firing fills a thin meter under the streak tally; a full meter makes the next volley a burst — 3× projectiles, 2× knockback, a deeper shoot sound and an ink ring (T0090) ✅
- Rest: after 30 s without taking any damage, the next discrete hit (enemy contact or enemy bullet, from any shape) is fully negated — no damage, no streak reset, no platemark (T0094) — and the 30 s count restarts from that moment; while covered a small-caps "REST" label holds in the HUD (the RHYTHM-flash idiom, T0014) and pops at the negation, which plays the existing low blip (T0101) instead of the hurt sound; continuous burns (the Gutter, the Crop, The Press) restart the count but are never negated (T0134) ✅
- Heavy burst: while any projectile of the last Inkwell burst is still on screen, your volley hits on bosses and elites deal +50% damage — saving the meter for the fight becomes a real decision (T0120, in queue, desktop-4070; the boss-stun half is held until after The Press (T0116) lands)
- Gutter: the first arena hazard — after 14:00 a 40-unit-wide ink band sweeps the crop-safe area horizontally every 20 s (4.0 s to cross, direction alternating); a thin red rule telegraphs it for 1.5 s; standing inside burns 4 damage/s (T0099) ✅
- Tide: the second arena hazard — after 16:00 the crop-safe boundary breathes: a 6 s period, the ink edge sliding 4% of the safe area inward over 3 s and back out over 3 s (linear triangular); while the ink advances, standing between it and the crop boundary burns 4 damage/s, the receding margin is safe; the crop-mark corners brighten toward full as the ink advances; the Gutter, The Press's wall and the spawn director all key off the same breathing rect, so nothing special-cases it (T0127, in queue, asus)
- Ink rain — the third arena hazard and the first on the clock: after 10:00 a small flat ink drop (radius 6, 150 u/s) falls every 1.4 s (±0.2 s seeded) at a random x in the crop-safe rect and splats into a 100-unit ink puddle (3.0 s, fading over the last 0.5 s) that slows the player 25% while their center is inside — the Wash idiom (T0111) at a stronger value, combining with other slows as two Wash puddles do; at most 8 live puddles, the drops themselves are weather (no damage, untargetable) — the page gets wet from above before the Gutter (14:00) and Tide (16:00) take over (T0137, in queue, narcissus)
- Gilt shards: 5% of all dropped shards are gilt — same shape, size and motion as a normal shard with a 2 u yellow outline and a small yellow center dot — and worth 3× XP; the shard economy gets a small gamble (T0112) ✅
- Shard glint: the moment a gilt shard is picked up, the pickup pluck plays one semitone higher and the XP actually granted prints as a small yellow "+N" pop at the pickup point — gilt is legible in the hand, not just on the page (T0117) ✅
- The Varnish: 10% of elite kills also drop a small flat yellow circle (radius 6, 2 u ink outline) that drifts toward you — picking it up coats your circle in a 2 u yellow outline for 8 s: all contact damage taken is halved (rounded down, after armor; bullets and continuous burns untouched) but shards in pickup range pop 60 u away and grant no XP until the coat ends — the elite loot becomes a decision per fight (T0139, in queue, narcissus)
- Overtime (refined in IDEAS; tasks on a strong machine after Tide (T0127) settles the late-run pacing): after 20:00 the page goes meaner and richer — enemy contact damage +50%, the spawn director 1.5× faster, all shards 2× XP; a small-caps "OVERTIME" note flashes in the HUD (the nameplate idiom, T0064) and the run tempo (T0111) jumps +10 BPM until death

## M3 — Bosses (started)
- The Frame at 5:00: health bar, charge + wedge ring + radial dart burst, +50% HP per respawn (T0006) ✅
- The Grid — second boss: a 400 HP rotating compass that sweeps two 200×12 ink arms (0.5 s red telegraph, 12 dmg) across the arena; boss content file + pure `gridSweep` timing module (unit-tested), reusing The Frame's boss infrastructure and +50% HP-per-respawn scaling (T0044) ✅
- The Grid's second voice: the moment The Grid's HP first drops below 50% of its (scaled) max HP the fight changes shape — it grows a third arm, same spec as its existing arms (200×12 ink, 12 damage, the same 0.5 s red telegraph), phase-locked 90° ahead of the first arm so the cross becomes a three-spoke asterisk with one empty quadrant, and spins 25% faster (×1.25) with no ramp; the second state latches for the rest of the fight (the 50% bar tick, T0118, prints at the same moment) and a respawned Grid starts fresh (T0132, in queue, asus)
- The Palette — third boss: five color swatches (red, orange, yellow, green, blue) fall down the page one at a time, 90 HP each, 6.0 s to fall, ≥120 units apart; each swatch you dodge paints a 2 u ring stripe on the player; rotation becomes Frame → Grid → Palette, extended to four bosses by T0116 (T0097) ✅
- Spectrum: for 8 s after The Palette dies, volleys print in the five swatch colors — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, one per projectile, wrapping — and a small-caps "SPECTRUM" note flashes in the HUD at the death; the boss's palette leaks into your fire (T0121, in queue, cachyos-x8664 — waits on T0119 and T0122)
- Boss shatter: the moment any boss dies, every enemy projectile on screen bursts into one normal shard at its position with a small flat ink ring — a boss kill is a breather and a payday (T0122, in queue, cachyos-x8664)
- The Press — the fourth boss: a printing press at the top of the page pushes a solid 70 u wall of ink down the page (1/150 of the safe height per second, plus a 1/20 stamp jump every 5 s behind a 1.0 s red-rule telegraph); you shoot the wall to damage the 520 HP boss, and each row of 4 type blocks (30 HP each) you clear pulls the wall back 1/16 of the safe height — the arena closes in around the fight (T0116, in progress, asus)
- Boss sting: a deep low hit + music swell on any boss spawn (T0058) ✅
- Boss nameplate intro: the first time each boss spawns, its name (from the boss content def, so The Palette gets it for free) is printed in small caps centered below the boss bar, fading over 2 s and freezing under overlays (T0064) ✅
- Boss tick: the boss health bar prints a small flat ink tick (2 u wide, ~6 u tall) at the 25/50/75% points as the boss first drops below each threshold, and the tick stays for that boss's life — a fresh spawn starts with a clean bar; how much of the fight is left is visible without counting (T0118) ✅
- Boss warning: 5 s before any boss spawn (Frame or Grid, including respawns) the arena boundary pulses red, "SOMETHING HEAVY APPROACHES" flashes in the HUD, and a low thud plays (T0070) ✅
- Boss music: while a boss is alive the score holds at a slow 64 BPM low pulse with a dissonant second voice (a semitone above the pulse, on alternating beats); it glides down over 1.5 s at the boss spawn event and back to the run tempo over 1.5 s on the boss's death (T0108) ✅
- Tempo change: the run tempo becomes `min(120, base curve + 2 × picks)` — every upgrade picked nudges the score +2 BPM, so a fast-building run reaches the 120 BPM clamp well before the 10:00 ramp; the nudge is suspended during the boss hold (T0108) and restored on boss death (T0115) ✅
- Boss payout line: Spectrum (T0121, cachyos) is first; Encore (The Frame: 10 s of 25% enemy slow + double shards) and Cadenza (The Grid: fire rate ×1.5 for 8 s) wait until Spectrum lands so all three payouts share one boss-death window (one machine, next cycle)
- Later: boss corpse decals

## M4 — Meta-progression
- Score: kills earn points (normal 1, elite 5, boss 50); HUD shows the running score, game over shows score + persisted best (T0054) ✅
- Kill feed: each kill pops a small ink "+1" / "+5" / "+50" above the corpse, matching the score values — "+1" small, "+5" larger and bolder, "+50" largest with a small ink ring at the boss corpse; pooled (~32 texts), ~0.6 s rise-and-fade like the damage numbers (T0106) ✅
- Rejection stamp: the game-over screen stamps the run's verdict in one word — a big red double-framed stamp (−8° tilt) that pops in 1.4× → 1.0× over 150 ms, 0.4 s after the card settles: "ERASED" under 8:00, "FRAMED" at 8:00+, "PUBLISHED" at 15:00+ (T0128, in queue, cachyos-x8664)
- Warm Start: after any run of 8:00+, the next run starts with 1 random upgrade level already applied — the first concrete step of the studio meta-progression (T0114) ✅
- Ghost run: the next run carries a faint pencil circle (2 u, no fill, 50% alpha) tracing your last run's path in sync with the run clock; the moment you outlive it, "PAST YOU" prints and the ghost holds at the spot where you died — the last run remains on the page (T0142, in queue, desktop)
- The Redactor: the first pre-run pick — after the first run ends, an ink-eraser glyph appears beside the start button; while picked, the run gets one active, Redact (Space / Y / a 64 px touch button, bottom right): it erases every enemy bullet within 140 u of the player and slows enemies in that radius by 40% for 2.5 s, 12 s cooldown shown as a thin ink ring draining clockwise around the button itself; the cost — every shard worth half XP — keeps it a decision per run, never a system that is simply always there (T0124, in queue, asus — unblocked now that the controls card (T0123) has landed; its Redact line goes on that card)
- Proof press: after any run of 10:00+ the menu lists a second pre-run pick — a starting weapon (Point, Line or Orbit) for the next run, applied at run start through the normal pick path so it shows in The Index (held: Warm Start (T0114) has landed, but the menu is hot — T0124's Redactor glyph and T0129's study cards both land there first; task after they settle; see IDEAS)
- The Gallery: three curated fixed-seed studies in the menu — Study in Red (only red and orange silhouettes: Wedge, Sentry, Splitter and their kin), No Level-Ups (no level bar, no cards, shards grant no XP, kills ×2 score), Double Shards (all shards 2× XP) — each with its own fixed seed and its own persisted best; a study differs from a normal run only in the stated rules (T0129, in queue, asus)
- Persistent currency between runs, a studio to unlock starting options
- Settings: the pause screen gets a SETTINGS card — MUSIC (OFF/ON, the existing M key stays in sync both ways), SFX (OFF/ON, a new key N listed on the controls card, T0123), SHAKE (OFF/ON, the T0040 camera shake; OFF = the camera never moves), FIRE (MANUAL/AUTO, AUTO fires continuously in the current aim direction, one-thumb friendly) — flat ink toggle buttons (active filled, inactive outlined), all four persisted in localStorage, defaults ON/ON/ON/MANUAL (T0140, in queue, narcissus)
